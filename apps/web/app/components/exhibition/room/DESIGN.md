# Walk-through exhibition room

This surface is an experience: visitors enter a navigable gallery and encounter the actual collection works at a believable physical scale. The room must read as architecture from its entry view, with floor, ceiling, side walls and a clear route visible together.

Use warm oak planks with visible grain, softly textured plaster, dimensional framing, and ceiling-mounted lights. Architecture uses physically lit materials; artwork reproductions preserve source colour. Surfaces need visible material detail at walking distance without large downloads or an unbounded per-work lighting cost.

Keep walking controls visible on desktop and touch. Drag to look, hold arrows to move/turn, select a work to approach it, and offer a return to the entrance. Preserve collision handling, reduced motion and the accessible Page template.

Wall plaques sit beyond the outer frame with a clear gap. Title, artist and catalogue details have separate hierarchy; descriptions wrap in full. Plaque height follows its actual contents. An absent description is not simulated with an empty panel or invented catalogue text. Saved AI descriptions remain editable and keep their authorship.

The Blender study is a separate architectural render. The interactive visitor surface is the actual Three.js scene, and must be verified in a browser, including movement, textures, framing and labels.
