── 1. the agent drafts ─────────────────────────────────────
title      : Weather at Sea [agent]
statement  : At sea, weather is not backdrop but force: mist obscures distance, wind redirects passage, and changing light makes the horizon unreliable. This room follows maritime experience through atmosphere rather than narrative, from the tense promise of a voyage to the small human improvisations demanded by uncertain conditions. Across drawing, print, and design, the sea appears less as a stable place than as a shifting field of visibility, risk, and desire. 
             [agent] 70 words
board      : 7 works
   · "--And mist or glim, I'd sail with him, if he would sail with me--"
     “Its paired phrases—mist or glim, sailing together—set departure between obscurity and a thin, unstable light. Pen, ink, and scraped corrections keep the promise of passage tentative.”
   · "'t Suynighe Waertje" and "De Goelicke Waerdin"
     “Two faces meet across separate oval frames, their names pairing sunny weather with a good hostess. Against the exhibition’s uncertain sea, this dry, hatched encounter suggests weather as a social condition as well as an atmospheric one.”
